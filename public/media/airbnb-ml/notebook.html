Airbnb Review Activity Prediction¶

A supervised machine learning study using the New York City Airbnb Open Data (2019) dataset to predict reviews_per_month from listing attributes. Monthly review activity is treated as a proxy for popularity, rather than a direct measure of bookings or revenue.

Recorded result: tuned Random Forest CV MAE 0.8660; test MAE 0.8656 reviews/month.

Scope: evaluation uses a random train/test split and excludes listings with missing targets. These results do not establish performance on future listings or zero-review listings.

Origin: this analysis originated as a CPSC 330 course project.

Data: New York City Airbnb Open Data, loaded from data/AB_NYC_2019.csv.
